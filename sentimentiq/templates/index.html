Model Selection Rationale

Logistic Regression achieved superior Macro F1 (75.69% vs 72.70%) and calibrated class probabilities. Its linear decision boundary coupled with L2 regularization effectively handles sparse TF-IDF n-gram vectors without the naive independence assumption present in Naive Bayes.

Sentiment-Aware NLP Preprocessing Pipeline

Inspect how raw customer feedback is normalized while preserving negation words.

1

Noise Stripping

Unescapes HTML entities, strips tags (), URLs (http/https), and @mentions.

2

Negation Preservation

Expands contractions (can't -> can not) and protects words like "not", "never", "no".

3

Tokenization & Case

Standardizes text to lowercase, strips non-sentiment symbols, and splits into word tokens.

4

TF-IDF Vectorization

Generates sublinear-scaled unigram and bigram features (1-2 ngrams) up to 5,000 vocabulary.
